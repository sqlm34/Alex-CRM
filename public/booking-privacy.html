Booking Privacy

Updated October 11, 2026

Alex Appliance Repair uses booking information to arrange and provide appliance repair visits, communicate about appointments, and maintain service records.

Booking information

Information you provide may include your name, phone number, email address, service address, appliance details, preferred appointment date and time, and uploaded appliance photographs. Please do not upload unrelated personal or sensitive information.

Email and calendar

We use your email address for booking acknowledgments, appointment confirmations, changes, cancellations, and visit reminders. Our email provider processes the address and message contents to deliver these communications.

When the company connects its Google Calendar, the booking system uses authorized access to create and update service appointment events and cancel them when the corresponding booking is canceled. Events may include the customer's name or email, service address, appliance type, appointment time, and service contact information. Google processes event information and invitations. Customers are not asked to give our website access to their personal Google Calendar.

Google authorization credentials are kept on the server, not in the public website. The integration is intended for service appointment management, not advertising. Google user data is not sold or used to train generalized AI models. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes and cancellation

Contact us to change or cancel a booking. Future queued reminders for a canceled booking are stopped; messages already delivered cannot be withdrawn. Calendar invitations and manually saved calendar copies behave differently. A manually saved copy must be updated or removed by you. Alert delivery depends on your calendar and device settings.

Service providers and records

Hosting, database, file storage, email, and calendar providers process information needed to operate these features. Access is limited to providing and administering the service. Service records may be retained for business recordkeeping; contact us with questions about access, correction, or deletion of your information.
